Privacy

Privacy

Today this is a static information site: no accounts, no forms that send data, no analytics, no ads, no cookies of our own. We do not sell your personal information and we do not publish raw case files.

What happens today

  • Hosting logs. The site is served by a third-party static host. Like any web host, it may log your IP address, browser and the pages requested to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not have access to a visitor list.
  • Email you send us. If you write to uapmontana@pm.me we keep the thread so we can answer and, for reports, investigate.

What we expect to add later

Each of these will be added to this page before it goes live.

  • Email list: email address, optional name, opens and clicks, through an email provider.
  • Merch checkout: order details held by the shop and payment vendor. We would not see full card numbers.
  • Sighting report form: date, time, location, description and optional contact details, stored in a secure, encrypted brand database with least-privilege access and no public API. Separate from any third-party case system.
  • Analytics: only if switched on, and a notice would appear.

Sighting reports

Reports are encouraged and investigated. “Investigated” is not “published”: report contents and contact details are never posted publicly. The online form is not live yet, so please don’t put witness personal details in an email you don’t need to. To ask for access to or deletion of something you sent, write to uapmontana@pm.me (subject to any legal obligation to keep it).

Sharing

We don’t sell lists or personal information. Processors we use (email host, and later list, shop and payment vendors) only handle data to provide their service. We may have to comply with lawful requests.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 where that is the rule), and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Questions: uapmontana@pm.me. Last updated 2026-10-02.
