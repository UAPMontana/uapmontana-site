Independent · public-source · Montana

What can be logged, checked, and falsified over Montana skies.

UAP Montana is an independent, public-source field-notes project. We log, check, and falsify, and we look for the truth outside government censorship. Field Notes is how the public-safe work ships. Not a detector shop. Not a case dump.

Public sources we watch
  • Fireball and meteor reports (AMS / IMO).
  • MADAR quiet vs. noisy framing, and space weather.
  • FAA GPS-test windows and open news.
  • Historical NUFORC patterns (an archive snapshot, not a live feed).

If a source is down, we say it is down. We do not fill a gap with a made-up orb.
