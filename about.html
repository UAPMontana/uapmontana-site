About UAP Montana

An independent, public-source field-notes project seeking the truth outside government censorship: what can be logged, checked, and falsified. Not a fan blog. Not an official agency mouthpiece.

Who we are

UAP Montana is an independent, public-source field-notes project: this site and its Field Notes. We look for the record, check the boring explanation first, and publish what survives daylight. We never invent a sighting to fill a hole. The project is run under the brand only, and we don’t publish personal details about the people behind it or about anyone who writes to us.

Independence

This site is independent and is not affiliated with any government agency. It does not publish confidential case files.

What we will cite

Public X and news, AMS/IMO fireball notices, MADAR quiet/noisy framing, space weather, FAA GPS-test windows, and historical NUFORC patterns. If a source is down, we say it’s down.

Reports

UFO / UAP reports are encouraged and all reports are investigated. A secure database will hold them, separate from any other organization’s systems. The public site never becomes a raw case dump. The intake form stays off until that database is real — see Contact.

What never goes up. Invented sightings. Witness personal details. Case-system dumps. “We have classified files” theater. A repurposed software demo passed off as a detector.

Independent. Public sources only. We do not invent sightings or publish confidential investigations.
